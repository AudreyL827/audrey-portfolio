Work

Some of these projects are finished. Some are still taking shape. They’re the ones I’ve spent the most time thinking through.

Writing these up one at a time. Nooki is next.

Projects

  • Nooki

    A gamified Pomodoro app. Every finished session builds one piece of furniture in a small clay room, and each piece is named with what you got done. Six rooms of 16 pieces, two timer modes, optional Google sign-in and Spotify, and it installs to a phone. Built with React and Firebase. Source on GitHub

    Live, case study in progress

  • Three.js portfolio room

    A black and white 3D room you move through instead of scroll, with an astronaut, a tesseract, candles, a wormhole, paintings, a female statue and an art deco cat. Navigation is closer to a small game than a website.

    In progress

  • Internal Fellowship Program partner package

    A file the UBC Product Management Club sends to companies so they can understand the fellowship program without a walkthrough. I worked on the content structure and visual hierarchy. Three pages of it are on the project page, next to an earlier draft of one of them.

    Written up

  • BUCS website

    The site for the BUCS community. I work on it in Figma with prototypes and Dev Mode, alongside the people building it. It has passed 2,000 visits.

    Live, ongoing

  • BUCS coffee-chat matching

    A feature to connect prospective students, current students and alumni more efficiently by matching them for coffee chats. I am product managing it.

    In progress
