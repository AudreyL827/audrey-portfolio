About

I study Business and Computer Science at UBC in Vancouver. Most of my time goes to two things: building products of my own, and running programs for student communities on campus.

A lot of my work starts with noticing what could feel simpler or more considered. Sometimes that’s a product. Sometimes it’s an event, my room, or dinner on a weeknight.

I also love photography, food, and travelling. For a while, most of my trips happened around fencing competitions. I would leave between classes, compete somewhere new, find something good to eat, take a few photos, and come home. I want to travel more, and eventually I’d like this site to hold those stories too.

Still writing this page. The work index is more current.

Right now

I am building Nooki, a gamified Pomodoro app where a finished focus session turns into collectible progress in a small room. Next to it I am modelling a 3D portfolio room in Three.js, which is the strangest thing on this site and the one I keep going back to.

On campus I am the Executive Development Program Manager for the UBC Product Management Club and Vice President of the BUCS Community Team. That means speakers, workshop decks, events, debriefs, and product work on the BUCS website and its coffee-chat feature.

Figma is where a lot of this starts. I use it for workshop decks, partner materials and website prototypes, and I hand work off through Dev Mode. I taught the beginner Figma workshop for the club using a Duolingo redesign. It is one of the tools I use, not the whole job.

Away from the screen I fence. I have competed, coached younger athletes and worked as a certified referee.
