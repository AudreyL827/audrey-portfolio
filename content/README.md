# content/

`dispatches.json` holds the agreed shape for a future **Dispatches** section:
photography, video, food, travel and everyday visual observations, gathered
under one heading instead of scattered across several top-level pages.

Nothing on the site reads this file. There is no Dispatches page, no navigation
link, and no placeholder entry. That is deliberate:

- The section goes public only when at least three real entries exist.
- Entries come from places Audrey actually went, meals she actually ate and
  photographs she actually took. No invented trips, restaurants, dates,
  competition results or memories.
- A gifted meal, an invitation or a paid collaboration is disclosed plainly on
  the entry itself, not in a footnote.

When it is time to build it, the navigation becomes `Work · Dispatches · About`,
and the three collections are Places, Table notes and Still frames. Photographs
carry the personality, so the postcard treatment stays on personal material
rather than being applied to every container.
